# Clinical Knowledge: Emergency Situations & Acute Symptoms Triage

## Life-Threatening Medical Emergencies
- Internal Bleeding: Symptoms include coughing up blood, vomiting material resembling coffee grounds, black/tarry stools, severe abdominal pain, dizziness, weakness, or fainting. Internal bleeding is a medical emergency requiring immediate hospitalization and emergency room evaluation.
- Acute Chest Pain / Suspected Heart Attack: Crushing chest pain, pressure radiating to jaw, neck, shoulder, or arm, shortness of breath, profuse sweating, and lightheadedness. Call emergency services immediately.
- Suspected Stroke: Sudden numbness/weakness of face, arm, or leg (especially one side), confusion, trouble speaking, difficulty seeing, or loss of balance (FAST protocol: Face, Arms, Speech, Time).
- Severe Breathing Difficulties / Anaphylaxis: Stridor, wheezing, swelling of the lips, tongue, or throat, hives, sudden drop in blood pressure.
- Severe Medication Overdose: Suspected ingestion of excessive dosage of any pharmaceutical product requires immediate emergency care or poison control contact.
