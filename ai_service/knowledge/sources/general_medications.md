# Clinical Knowledge: Common Medications & Indications

## Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
- Indications: Mild-to-moderate pain relief (headache, toothache, muscular aches) and reduction of fever.
- Standard Dosage: Adults: 500mg to 1000mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Maximum daily dose: 4000mg (4 grams) in 24 hours.
- Safety Warnings: Overdose can lead to severe and potentially fatal liver toxicity. Check all co-administered cold or pain medications to ensure they do not also contain paracetamol.

## Augmentin (Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium)
- Indications: Broad-spectrum antibacterial combination for bacterial infections including sinusitis, otitis media, lower respiratory tract infections, skin/soft tissue infections, and urinary tract infections.
- Administration: Take with food or at the start of a meal to enhance absorption and minimize GI discomfort.
- Warnings: Contraindicated in patients with a history of severe penicillin hypersensitivity or cholestatic jaundice/hepatic dysfunction associated with Augmentin.

## Artemether / Lumefantrine (ACT)
- Indications: First-line oral artemisinin-based combination therapy for uncomplicated Plasmodium falciparum malaria.
- Administration: Take with high-fat meals or whole milk to maximize bioavailability and clinical efficacy.

## Metformin
- Indications: First-line oral biguanide antihyperglycemic agent for Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus.
- Administration: Take with meals to reduce gastrointestinal adverse effects (nausea, diarrhea, abdominal cramps).
